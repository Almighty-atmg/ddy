02 • Screen Design Specifications

UI Layout & Heritage Styling

  • Color System: Deep Awadhi Emerald (#164d36), Forest Ink (#0d3022), Zardozi Gold (#b99350), Kesari Saffron (#df6915), and Parchment Ivory (#f8f4e9).
  • Typography: Cormorant Garamond for royal editorial headings paired with DM Sans for crisp HUD counters, ingredient labels, and leaderboards.
  • Responsive Viewport: Full-screen adaptive grid layout that scales seamlessly across wide desktop monitors, tablets, and Android landscape/portrait screens without artificial phone bezels.
